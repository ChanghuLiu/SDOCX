# Privacy

All conversion is performed locally on the Android device. Files are not
uploaded or shared. The app has no account, analytics, advertising, backend, or
Internet permission. Users explicitly choose source files and the output ZIP
destination through Android Storage Access Framework. Temporary cache files may
exist during conversion and are cleaned on normal completion, cancellation, or
failure, and stale conversion cache is cleaned when the app starts; users
should not treat the cache as permanent storage.
