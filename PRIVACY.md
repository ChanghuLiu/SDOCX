# Privacy

All document conversion is performed locally on the Android device. Files are not
uploaded or shared. The app does not require an account or use a developer
backend. Users explicitly choose source files and the output ZIP destination
through Android Storage Access Framework. Temporary cache files may exist during
conversion and are cleaned on normal completion, cancellation, or failure, and
stale conversion cache is cleaned when the app starts; users should not treat
the cache as permanent storage.

When a Firebase project is configured for the app, Google Analytics for Firebase
collects pseudonymous app-instance analytics events to help the developer
understand app opens and the conversion workflow (such as the selected stage,
whether a source picker was cancelled, and whether a conversion completed,
failed, or was cancelled). These events do not include document names, paths,
contents, selected file URIs, or free-text errors. Firebase Analytics may also
automatically collect app-instance and app/device technical information as
described by Google. Data is processed by Google under its privacy terms:
https://policies.google.com/privacy. Analytics is inactive in builds without
the app's Firebase configuration. App users do not need to create or sign in to
a Firebase account.
