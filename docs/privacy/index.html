Notes Escape: SDOCX

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-14

Notes Escape: SDOCX is an independent migration utility for converting user-selected Samsung Notes .sdocx exports into portable Markdown / Obsidian-compatible archives.

Local processing

Conversion runs locally on your Android device. Files are not uploaded, sent to a server, or processed in the cloud. The app does not require an account and does not include a backend, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect personal data or share data with third parties. The app has no Internet permission. You explicitly choose the source files and the destination ZIP using Android’s file picker.

Temporary files

During conversion, the app may create temporary cache files on the device so one note can be processed at a time. These files are cleaned after successful completion, cancellation, or failure, and stale conversion cache is cleaned when the app starts. Uninstalling the app removes its app-local data according to Android’s normal app storage behavior.
